**Time permitting · Windows, macOS, or Linux · .NET 10 + Node.js 22 or 24**

Build a console application that accepts a webpage URL, opens it with Playwright, identifies accessibility problems, consults an application-owned WCAG catalog, and produces a structured report.

Begin in `labs/04-copilot-sdk/start/HelloCopilotSDK`. Compiling checkpoints and completed samples are included beside it for recovery and comparison.
